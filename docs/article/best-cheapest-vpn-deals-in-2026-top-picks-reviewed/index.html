When hunting for the **cheapest VPN deals** in 2026, it's crucial to understand that "cheap" shouldn't equate to "compromised." While saving money is a priority, the primary purpose of a VPN – security and privacy – must remain intact.   • Robust Security & Privacy Features: This is non-negotiable. Look for VPNs that offer industry-standard AES-256 encryption, a strict no-logs policy (preferably independently audited), and a reliable kill switch to prevent data leaks if your connection drops. Check for providers with a decent number of servers globally and unlimited bandwidth. While ExpressVPN often comes at a premium, some providers manage to offer excellent speeds at lower price points.

Many budget-friendly VPNs now offer 5, 7, or even unlimited simultaneous connections, making them excellent value for families or individuals with multiple gadgets. Surfshark is a prime example, often providing unlimited connections at a very attractive price.

This is particularly important with cheaper options, ensuring you can get a refund if the service doesn't meet your expectations. VPN Unlimited (KeepSolid) also offers this flexibility.

Ultimately, the best value often comes from longer subscription plans, so factor that into your search for the absolute **cheapest VPN deals**.
