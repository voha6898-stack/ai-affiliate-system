How much do online creative writing courses cost?

The cost of online creative writing courses varies widely depending on the provider, course length, and level of instruction. What are the most popular online courses for creative writing?

Yes, many online creative writing courses offer a certificate of completion upon finishing the course. How long do online creative writing courses typically last?

The length of online creative writing courses varies depending on the provider and course structure. Not all online creative writing courses are accredited, but many reputable providers offer courses that are accredited by recognized accrediting agencies.
